# Meetos demo video — script and shot list (2:58)

Record the **live site** (https://usemeetos.vercel.app) in desktop Chrome at 1920×1080.
Everything you show must be real: real Gemini results and real labels.

## Before you press record (10 minutes, off camera)

1. In a fresh workspace (Settings → Workspace → Create another workspace, name it
   `Brightside Bakery`), add and **analyse** meeting 1 and meeting 2 from
   `docs/DEMO_TRANSCRIPTS.md` (dates 16 Nov and 23 Nov 2026, 10:00).
2. Have `docs/test-data/website-relaunch-readiness-review.txt` ready in your Downloads folder.
3. Schedule a meeting `Website relaunch check-in` for 1 Dec 2026, 10:00 (New meeting →
   Schedule only), participants Ada Mensah and Tunde Bello.
4. Close other tabs, hide bookmarks (Ctrl+Shift+B), zoom the page to 110% (Ctrl and +).
5. Do one full practice run. Analysis takes some time — you can cut the waiting in editing.

## Shot list and voiceover

The **VO** lines are the voiceover. Read them calmly; each scene's time is a guide.

### 1 · 0:00–0:12 — Hook (landing page)
**Show:** the landing page; slowly scroll to the Commitment Graph section.
**VO:** "Meetings shouldn't end with notes. They should end with work getting done. This is Meetos."

### 2 · 0:12–0:24 — The problem
**Show:** the Commitment Graph section of the landing page.
**VO:** "Every meeting creates promises: who will do what, and by when. Most of them get lost. Meetos remembers every commitment and follows it across meetings, until a person confirms it's done."

### 3 · 0:24–0:40 — Add a meeting
**Show:** Meetings → New meeting → **Upload file** → choose the readiness review file. Title
`Website relaunch launch readiness review`, date 24 Nov 2026, 15:00. Click **Add as
participants** (6 people appear). **Save meeting**.
**VO:** "You can record a meeting live, with everyone's consent, or upload a transcript. Here's a forty-minute launch review with six people. Meetos finds the speakers automatically."

### 4 · 0:40–1:00 — Hybrid AI analysis
**Show:** click **Analyse**. Show the 5 parts progressing and their labels. (Cut the waiting.)
**VO:** "Analysis runs through Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference. When this device can run Gemini Nano, the transcript never leaves it. Otherwise Gemini in the cloud takes over. Every part is labelled with where it really ran — no guessing."

### 5 · 1:00–1:20 — Results with evidence
**Show:** Decisions tab, then Commitments tab (owners and dates), click one item's evidence quote.
**VO:** "Out come decisions, commitments, owners and deadlines. 'By Friday' becomes a real date. Every item quotes the exact line it came from, and anything the AI can't back up is thrown away."

### 6 · 1:20–1:45 — The Commitment Graph
**Show:** Commitments tab → **Earlier commitments discussed here**. Open the **menu prices** commitment
→ Commitment Graph and timeline. Then open the **menu photos** commitment → "Deadline moved".
**VO:** "This is the Commitment Graph. Meetos linked this meeting to promises from the last two weeks: a deadline that moved, a blocker, and a task someone says is finished."

### 7 · 1:45–1:55 — A person confirms
**Show:** on the menu prices commitment: "A later meeting says this is done. Confirm?" → confirm.
**VO:** "But the AI never closes a task. A person confirms it — and the database enforces that."

### 8 · 1:55–2:07 — Decision memory
**Show:** Decisions page → "Gift cards move to February" replacing "Gift cards wait until January".
**VO:** "Decisions are remembered too: who agreed, and when a later decision replaced an earlier one."

### 9 · 2:07–2:22 — Memory
**Show:** Memory → type `What happened to the launch date?` → answer with sources. Then type
`What did we decide about office parking?` → "Not found in your workspace".
**VO:** "Ask your meetings anything. Answers come only from your records, with sources. And if it was never discussed, Meetos says so instead of making something up."

### 10 · 2:22–2:35 — Prepare
**Show:** Prepare → choose `Website relaunch check-in` → outstanding and overdue items →
**Generate** agenda.
**VO:** "Before the next meeting, Meetos prepares a briefing: what's overdue, what's blocked, and a suggested agenda."

### 11 · 2:35–2:45 — Execution and Daily Brief
**Show:** Execution page, then Overview → Daily Brief.
**VO:** "Execution shows how commitments are really going, from real records — and the Daily Brief tells you what needs attention today."

### 12 · 2:45–2:52 — Privacy
**Show:** Settings → AI & privacy → the three modes (Hybrid, On-device only, Cloud only).
**VO:** "You choose where AI runs. On-device only means nothing is sent to a cloud model."

### 13 · 2:52–2:58 — Close
**Show:** back to the landing page headline.
**VO:** "Meetos. Meetings that end with work getting done. Built with Firebase AI Logic."
