# Demo transcripts (fictional)

Two **fictional** meetings written to exercise every MEETOS feature: decisions with explicit
agreement, commitments with owners and spoken deadlines, open questions, and a follow-up meeting
that reports progress, a blocker, a moved deadline, a "done" claim and a changed decision.
The people and the bakery are made up. Use them for testing and demos; the app stores
whatever you paste exactly as you paste it.

**How to use:** Meetings → **New meeting** → set the title and **Date & time** below → paste
the transcript → **Add as participants** (adds the four speakers) → Create → **Analyse**.
Create meeting 1 first and analyse it, then meeting 2. Deadlines such as "by Friday" are
resolved from the meeting date, so the dates matter.

---

## Meeting 1 — Website relaunch kickoff

- **Title:** Website relaunch kickoff
- **Date & time:** Monday 16 November 2026, 10:00

```text
Ada Mensah: Okay, let's start. The goal today is to agree the plan for the Brightside Bakery website relaunch.
Grace Kim: I've got two directions for the homepage. A photo-led one and an illustrated one.
Tunde Bello: The photo-led one is easier for us. We already have the product shots.
Sam Ortiz: Illustrated looks nice, but it would take longer to produce.
Ada Mensah: Then let's decide. We'll go with the photo-led homepage. Does everyone agree?
Grace Kim: Agreed.
Tunde Bello: Agreed.
Sam Ortiz: Yes, agreed.
Ada Mensah: Second thing, the launch date. I'd like to launch on December 4.
Sam Ortiz: December 4 works for the newsletter.
Ada Mensah: Then that's decided. The launch date is December 4.
Ada Mensah: Grace, can you finish the homepage design?
Grace Kim: Yes, I'll finish the homepage design by Friday.
Tunde Bello: I'll build the online ordering page by next Wednesday.
Sam Ortiz: I'll write the launch newsletter by next Thursday.
Ada Mensah: And I'll send the menu prices to Tunde tomorrow.
Tunde Bello: One open question. Do we need gift cards at launch, or can they wait?
Ada Mensah: I don't know yet. I'll check with the owner this week.
Sam Ortiz: Also, who is taking the photos for the menu page?
Ada Mensah: Let's leave that open for now. Thanks, everyone.
```

## Meeting 2 — Website relaunch weekly sync

- **Title:** Website relaunch weekly sync

```text
Ada Mensah: Quick check-in on the website relaunch. Grace, how is the homepage design?

Ada Mensah: Great. Tunde, how is the online ordering page?
```

## What to expect (and show in the demo)

After meeting 1:
- **Decisions:** photo-led homepage (agreed by Grace, Tunde, Sam); launch on December 4.
- **Commitments:** Grace — homepage design (Fri 20 Nov); Tunde — ordering page (Wed 25 Nov);
  Sam — newsletter (Thu 26 Nov); Ada — menu prices (Tue 17 Nov); Ada — check gift cards with
  the owner.
- **Open questions:** gift cards at launch? who takes the menu photos?

After meeting 2 (Commitment Graph linking):
- *Homepage design* — a "done" claim; MEETOS asks a person to confirm it.
- *Online ordering page* — blocked (missing menu prices), deadline moved to Friday 27 Nov.
- *Menu prices* — repeated and overdue since 17 Nov.
- *Newsletter* — progress update, still on track.
- Decision "launch December 9" replaces "launch December 4"; gift cards wait until January.
- New commitments: Sam updates the newsletter (Wed 25 Nov); Grace shoots menu photos (Thu 26 Nov).

The AI decides what it extracts, so details can differ from this list, but every saved
item must quote the transcript.

**Memory questions to try:** "What happened to the launch date?", "Why is the ordering page
late?", "What did we decide about gift cards?"

**Prepare:** create a third meeting (e.g. "Website relaunch check-in", 30 November) and open
**Prepare** for it to see outstanding and overdue commitments and the changed decisions.
